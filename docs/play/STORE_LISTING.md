## Full description (4000 max)

Fariin is a messenger for the people you care about. Send messages, photos and voice notes, make
voice and video calls, share stories and follow channels.

Private by design
Your one to one messages and calls are end to end encrypted. They are locked on your phone before
they leave it and only the person you are talking to can open them. We cannot read them.

Chats
- Text, photos, videos and voice messages
- Reactions, replies, edits and delete for everyone
- See when a message is sent, delivered and read
- Drafts and messages you send offline go out when you are back online

Calls
- Voice and video calls between two people, end to end encrypted
- Join group calls from a chat
- Calls with someone you have not chatted with are always relayed, so your IP address stays hidden

Groups
- Group chats with admins, photos and names
- Add friends and start talking in seconds

Stories
- Share a photo or video that disappears after a time you pick, from 6 to 48 hours
- Choose who can see each story

Channels
- Follow channels for news and updates from people and groups you trust

Your account, your control
- Sign in with email, Google or a passkey
- Choose who can message you, who sees your last seen, and who can call you
- Block and report from any chat
- Fariin never reads your contacts and has no ads

Fariin is for people aged 16 and over.
